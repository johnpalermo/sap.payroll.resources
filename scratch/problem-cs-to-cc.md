# 9B17 and maternity leave — this document does answer it.

- 9B17 is "Adj to YTD Base Previous Year", the adjustment wage type for 9B16 (paired with 9B07 for 9B06), enterable as a one-time payment.
- It is also generated by the annualization routine that runs with the January 1 calculation for employees on LWOP: 9B17 = 9B06 ÷ (365 − days LWOP) × days LWOP. It grosses the prior year's base earnings up for the unpaid days so the two-year average is not depressed by the leave. Maternity is not named there, but the maternity/parental top-up and waiting-period wage types (0009–0011, 0030–0035, 0040–0042, 0980–0982, 1612, 1642) cumulate through 9B11 into 9B06, and the unpaid remainder of the leave is what the annualization addresses.
- Three cautions. The worked figure does not reproduce from the stated formula: 15,000 ÷ 300 × 65 = 3,250.00, not 3,358.33, so the rule itself has to be checked. The accumulator table swaps 9B15/9B17 between the 9B14 and 9B16 rows (and mislabels 9B16 as 9B12); the narrative is the reliable version. And the document says leave logic was later changed by SCRs ("need to add logic for other leaves", SCR 8 rotation suppression), so production behaviour on leave may differ from this text.

This spec changes my earlier read in one important way. Under the first document the two-year average only applied at two or more years of service, so I said the recast probably showed no difference before 2026‑01‑01. This document defines the under-two-years average as the greatest of 9B06 alone, (9B06 + current base) ÷ 2 and (9B06 + 9B16) ÷ 2, and applies it in the annual calculation as well; the worked example confirms it (100,000 at 1 Jan 2005 with 1 year 10 months of service, measured from hire). For a CSD of 2023‑09‑19:

- Rehire: base = IT0008 base salary including draw (0001 + 0005), same as CS, unless bases or CSD carried over from the previous employment.
- Pension eligibility event in 2024 (nightly calculation, only if not on leave): base salary almost certainly wins, since 9B06 holds only a few months of 2024.
- 2025‑01‑01 (1 year 3 months service): greatest of base salary, full-year 2024 9B06 (salary + commissions + top-ups), the two averages, and the salary-range minimum. If 2024 total base earnings exceeded salary, the divergence starts here, a year earlier than I said.
- 2026‑01‑01 (2 years 3 months): (9B06 for 2025 + 9B16 for 2024) ÷ 2, with 9B17 annualization if there were LWOP days in 2025.

Also useful: the accumulators are country-wide (MOLGA 07), not subgroup-dependent, so the stored 9B06/9B16 already contain any commission paid under a wage type in the 9B11 list (1403, 1729, 1736, 1737 and so on). The as-is values can be used directly, provided the employee's commission wage type is on that list.

Changes to the data list for the employee:

- Rehire action type and reason (MASSN 71 / MASSG). Rule 9B00 empties the buckets on rehire outside the window (42 days in the rule table, 90 in the narrative), which determines whether 9B16 at 2024‑01‑01 held pre-termination earnings and whether old base/old base salary carried forward.
- IT9171 full history with all fields, especially benbseind, the eligibility override indicators, ltdind, GRTW and penjoindt. A manual override value stops the batch recalculation entirely.
- IT0008 by wage type (0001, 0005, 0006), not just the annual salary field.
- Position start date from IT0001 and the salary-range minimum at each recalculation point, since the floor also applies with under two years of position experience.
- Leave detail: type and dates of every leave since rehire, LWOP days per calendar year, WPBP employment status 1/2, and top-up wage types paid per period.
- CRT snapshot of 9B06, 9B16, 9B07, 9B17, 9B10, 9B11, 9B42, 9B52 at the last pay of 2023, 2024, 2025 and current; 9B01 if any period had less than full-time hours.
- RT per period since eligibility: 9B08, 9B24, 9B09, EE and ER pension wage types, RESSOP EE and ER wage types.
- The commission and variable wage types actually paid to the employee, which I will match against the 9B10/9B11/9B42 lists.

Gaps that remain:

1. How 9B52 enters the per-pay contribution base: the documented chain is 9B09 = 9B01 + 9B08 only, yet Pension Earnings is defined as base plus 9B52. Wage type 1736 (Commission-pensionable) cumulates to both 9B11 (base) and 9B42 (prior-year STI), which is a double-count risk for a commissioned employee unless the contribution rule excludes it.
2. Leave handling at January 1: the general note says no bases are generated on leave, while the annual section only exempts sick leave and LTD (with LWOP annualization still running). The IT9171 records around the January 1 following the maternity leave will show what the system actually did.
3. The RESSOP contribution rule is still absent; this document defines bases, not the savings-plan formula.

One methodological consequence worth noting for later: the accumulators rebuild themselves on retro (cumulations restart from the prior CRT and cross-year rotation is redone), but IT9171 does not. A hybrid of inserting the should-be IT9171 base history and letting payroll retro re-derive 9B08/9B09 and the contribution differences is mechanically supported by this design.
